Who we are

HERizon Health is a student‑led organization advancing women’s health through education, advocacy, community outreach, and health equity.

We focus on making healthcare easier to navigate for women and girls—especially those facing barriers like cost, language, bias, or lack of information.

Why this toolkit matters

Healthcare access isn’t equal for everyone. Social and economic factors, bias, and under‑representation in research all affect how women are treated and heard.

This toolkit gives you clear, simple steps to:

  • Find care, even with limited money or insurance
  • Prepare for appointments so your symptoms are taken seriously
  • Stay calmer and more confident in stressful health situations

HERizon Toolkit

Tap any tool to see how it can support you. Everything is designed to be simple, practical, and easy to use.

Hotline
Call a trusted hotline when you feel unsafe, overwhelmed, or need immediate support. You deserve to feel protected and heard, and talking to someone can help you decide your next step.
Low‑Cost Clinic
Look for community clinics that offer affordable or free care. They can help with exams, birth control, STI testing, pregnancy care, and more—even if you don’t have insurance.
Symptom Tracker
Write down what you’re feeling, when it started, and what makes it better or worse. Bringing notes to an appointment helps providers understand your experience and take it seriously.
Translation Help
Use translation apps or tools to understand medical forms, instructions, or conversations. Clear language is a right, not a privilege—this can help you feel more confident and informed.
